Assistance & Policies

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential information regarding our sizing specifications, worldwide delivery, returns, and textile preservation.

1. Sizing, Fit & Alterations

Our silhouettes feature intentional architectural tailoring with comfortable ease. Outerwear is drafted with dropped shoulders and relaxed drape to accommodate chunky knitwear. If you prefer a closer, traditional fit, we recommend selecting one size down. Full measurements are available on each product page and within our Size Guide.

Yes. Clients in Paris and New York may schedule a private fitting at our salons for custom hem adjustments, sleeve alterations, and personalized fit refinement. For online clients, we provide generous fabric allowances in hem cuffs to facilitate local luxury alterations.

2. Shipping & Customs Duties

We deliver worldwide via DHL Express Carbon-Neutral Courier to over 90 countries. Orders over $250 receive complimentary express shipping. Delivery typically takes 2–3 business days within Western Europe and North America, and 3–5 days to Asia Pacific and Australia.

Yes. All shipments to the United States, European Union, United Kingdom, Switzerland, and Japan are dispatched on a DDP (Delivery Duty Paid) basis. There will be no surprise carrier fees upon delivery.

3. Returns & Exchanges

We offer complimentary 30-day returns and exchanges on all unworn garments with original atelier security tags and garment bags intact. Simply contact our concierge team at concierge@maisonvelene.com to receive a pre-paid return shipping label and scheduled courier pickup.

4. Garment Care & Preservation

Cashmere is a self-cleaning natural fiber that benefits greatly from resting between wears. We advise hanging your coat on broad wooden hangers in a well-ventilated area. Spot clean small marks with a damp cloth, and use a specialist green dry cleaner once per season. Avoid plastic dry-cleaning bags and store in our breathable organic linen garment bags.
