Data Protection & Governance

Privacy Policy & Terms

Our commitment to safeguarding your private client information and maintaining ethical transparency across our global atelier operations.

1. Private Client Data Collection

At Maison Vélène, we treat your privacy with the same discretion and restraint that defines our tailoring. We collect only the essential information required to fulfill your orders, provide private fitting consultations, and personalize your experience. This includes your name, shipping address, email, phone number, and transaction records.

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal data with external third parties or data brokers.

2. Security & Payment Processing

All transactions conducted through our digital storefront are processed using bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption adhering to Level 1 PCI-DSS compliance standards. Payment information is tokenized and transmitted directly to certified banking gateways; our servers never store raw credit card numbers.

3. Cookie Governance & Analytics

We utilize minimal functional cookies strictly necessary to maintain your shopping bag across browsing sessions and remember your preferred currency. Performance cookies are deployed anonymously to optimize loading speeds and device responsiveness.

4. Terms of Service & Authenticity

Every Maison Vélène silhouette is protected under international intellectual property and industrial design laws. All garments are accompanied by an atelier certificate of authenticity. Unauthorized reproduction or commercial resale without explicit written consent is strictly prohibited.

5. Contacting Our Data Governance Officer

For inquiries regarding the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), or to request full deletion of your private client account, please contact our Legal Directorate at privacy@maisonvelene.com.
